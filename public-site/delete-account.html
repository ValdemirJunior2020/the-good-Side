Delete Your Account

Instructions for deleting your Good Side account and associated app data.

Delete in the iOS app

Sign in, open You → Data & Security → Delete My Account and Data, and confirm the deletion request. The app requests deletion of your Good Side profile, preferences, saved stories and related records. An email sign-in identity is also scheduled for deletion through the authentication service; your separately managed Google identity is not affected.

Delete from the website

Sign in at My Account, scroll to Security & account → Delete my account and data, then confirm using the instructions shown there.

If something doesn't work

Send an email to infojr.83@gmail.com from the email associated with your account. We may need to verify the request to protect your account. Do not include your password or sign-in code.

What is and isn't deleted

The deletion feature is intended to remove your Good Side account and related profile and preference data. It does not delete your separate Google account, original publisher accounts, or Apple device settings. Operational records may be held for limited periods where security, backup or legal obligations require them. If a backend deletion fails, support can help finish the request.
