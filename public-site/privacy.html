Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · How The Good Side handles account and app information.

Who we are

The Good Side is a positive-news discovery website and iOS app. For privacy questions, contact infojr.83@gmail.com. Our website is thegoodsidenews.com.

Information we process

When you sign up or sign in, we process account identifiers, your email address, display name, and sign-in provider. If you use Google sign-in, Google may supply basic profile information. We process selected news interests, saved stories and recipes, avatar choices, notification preferences, preferred notification times and timezone. For opted-in push notifications, we process device push tokens and delivery status information. Our hosting and authentication systems may also process technical network, security, diagnostic and service-request records.

Why we use your information

We use these details to sign you in securely, operate the app, personalize your positive-news feed, restore saved choices, send notifications you request, respond to support requests, and protect the service against misuse. News browsing does not require permission for push notifications.

Providers and publishers

We use services such as Supabase for account authentication and supporting data, Render for app APIs, Netlify for website hosting, Google for optional identity sign-in, and Expo/Apple for optional push delivery. News may contain links to independent publishers, and images may be fetched from external HTTPS locations. When you open an external publisher, that publisher's own privacy practices apply. We do not control third-party websites.

Your choices

You can edit your account preferences, notification schedule, saved stories and avatar in the app. You can disable push notifications in iOS Settings. You may request access to account information, correction or deletion by contacting our support email.

Deletion and retention

Where account deletion is available, open the app's You → Data & Security → Delete My Account and Data option or visit our account-deletion instructions. Account deletion is intended to remove your Good Side account and associated app records. It does not delete an independent Google account or external publisher account. Some operational logs, support correspondence, backups or records may remain for limited periods where required to operate systems, protect security or comply with law. If deletion fails, contact support for manual assistance.

Security

We use authenticated services, access controls and HTTPS where available, but no internet service can guarantee perfect security. Do not send passwords or login codes in support emails.

Children and international usage

The Good Side is not presented as a child-directed service. Service providers may process information in different countries based on their infrastructure. Additional requirements may apply to children or to users in particular regions.

Changes

We may revise this policy as product features or providers change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Questions: infojr.83@gmail.com.
